Credit Report Review and Disputes

Help reviewing your credit reports and disputing information that may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or unverifiable.

What we review

Common Credit Report Issues

We help you find items that may be reported incorrectly and dispute them with the credit bureaus and creditors.

Late payments reported in error

Payments marked late that you believe were on time.

Collections reported incorrectly

Collection accounts with wrong amounts, dates or owners.

Identity theft accounts

Accounts opened in your name that you never authorized.

Incorrect balances

Balances or limits that don't match your records.

Duplicate accounts

The same debt listed more than once.

Outdated items

Negative items reported past the time allowed by law.

Process

How It Works

  1. Review your reports

    We go through your credit reports with you and note items that may be inaccurate, incomplete or outdated.

  2. File disputes

    Disputes go to the credit bureaus and creditors, with the details and records that support them.

  3. Track and follow up

    We track responses and explain results and next steps, including habits that support healthy credit.

Know your rights. You can dispute errors directly with the credit bureaus yourself, for free. No one can legally remove accurate, timely information from your credit report. Results depend on the facts and on the bureaus' investigations, so no removal or score increase is promised.

FAQ

Credit Questions

How long does it take?

Credit bureaus generally have about 30 days to investigate a dispute. How things turn out depends on your reports and the bureaus' findings, so we don't promise a timeline or a score.

Can you remove negative items?

Only information that is inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or can't be verified can be corrected or removed. Accurate, timely information stays on your report.

What do I need to get started?

Request a consultation first. Please don't upload credit reports or account numbers through the website or chat; Robert will explain how to share them.
